This is an update and rework of the mod Natures Pretty Sweet. Split up into multiple mods for convenience.
Originally created by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1211694919]tkkntkkns[/url]
Then continued by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3542949511]Mlie[/url]

This mod has been split into three parts, Weather Effects, Biomes, [b]Snow textures[/b].

When I was looking at the textures the original NPS had, there were a decent amount of snow textures for base game trees. Rather than have them as new textures, I cut out the plant part and repurposed them as overlays. It didn't seem appropriate in the weather effects mod, so I put them into its own mod.

This adds snow overlay textures to a bunch of base game trees and plants. Trees like birch, oak, and willow. As well as brambles, chokevine and a couple other plants.
There is an option to replace the base games snow overlay textures on some trees with this mods. It is off by default.
Will possibly look weird with plant retexture mods. Not much I can do about that.

Should be safe to add/remove mid-save
